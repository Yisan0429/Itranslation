The relationship between pressure and volume is given by the ideal gas law: $$PV = nRT$$
where P is pressure (Pa), V is volume (m³), n is the number of moles, R is the gas constant (8.314 J/mol·K), and T is temperature (K).

Key assumptions:
- Gas particles have negligible volume
- No intermolecular forces
- Collisions are perfectly elastic

For further details, see https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetic_theory_of_gases.
